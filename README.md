## Featured builds

### ⚡ [XL6019 adjustable boost converter](https://github.com/lithiumvision/xl6019-boost-converter)

A custom DC–DC step-up board designed around the XL6019, with an adjustable output targeting approximately 30 V. The repository includes the schematic, PCB views, Proteus source, and Gerber/CAM exports.

**Focus:** power conversion · PCB layout · fabrication files

### 🔋 [Dual-rail breadboard power supply](https://github.com/lithiumvision/breadboard-power-supply-5v-3.3v)

A compact supply project for breadboard prototyping, with selectable 5 V and 3.3 V rails, USB and DC-jack input paths, and status indicators. Includes schematic and PCB material plus Proteus projects.

**Focus:** regulated supplies · circuit design · prototyping

## How I work

- **Design with a purpose:** document the circuit and the choices behind it.
- **Make it buildable:** keep the schematic, PCB views, and project files together.
- **Check the real hardware:** compare the assembled circuit with the design and record what still needs measuring.
